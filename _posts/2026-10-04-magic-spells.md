</figure><p>A friend of mine has a concept called &#8220;magic spells&#8221;. </p><p>Sometimes you&#8217;re blocked on something, you can unlock it with a specific string of words &#8211;&nbsp;a magic spell. Saying it is <em>simple</em>, but rarely <em>easy</em>, which is why you haven&#8217;t said it yet.</p><p>His example: a friend on a retreat was interested in someone but couldn&#8217;t quite get their attention, wasn&#8217;t sure how to move things forward. The location was already pretty romantic &#8211; a big house in the countryside surrounded by woods. He suggested the following magic spell: &#8220;want to go for a walk with me?&#8221;</p><p>It worked, eventually, though it wasn&#8217;t straightforward (such things rarely are). In one sentence he signalled his interest, put his intention out into the world.</p><p>The words alone don&#8217;t solve your problem. But once they&#8217;re out there in the world, you&#8217;ve set the points, like a switch on a railway line, and your life now defaults to running down a different track.</p><p>In the past I&#8217;ve set aside an hour to find magic spells. Get angry, feel the irritation bubble up inside you at how things are so <em>suboptimal</em>. There&#8217;s a better life out there, one that brings you joy and ease, if only you were bold enough to reach for it.</p><p>Then write the list. What things can I do <em>now</em> that will improve my life, that will put me on a better path?</p><p>Three things stand out as substantially changing the direction of my life:</p><ul><li><p>Messaging the person who ran the group house I was looking at to say I wanted to take the room there. This put me in a much happier and healthier place, in a wonderful community. It led to me hosting parties, starting a new relationship, making new friends, making some amazing memories.</p></li><li><p>Texting my then-girlfriend to say we need to talk about our relationship &#8211; taking a hint from Cate Hall about <a href="https://usefulfictions.substack.com/p/are-you-stuck-in-movie-logic">not living in movie logic</a>, where you <a href="https://usefulfictions.substack.com/p/are-you-stuck-in-movie-logic#:~:text=Tip%203%3A%20Name,what%20it%20is.%E2%80%9D">don&#8217;t even need to be able to precisely say what the problem is</a> as long as you can say that <em>there is a problem</em>. The difficult conversation had been coming for a long time but neither of us could summon the courage. Once the words were said, we were on a path to talking about it.</p></li><li><p>Signing up to <a href="https://www.thehum.org/courses-and-events/fight-wise%3A-find-your-backbone">Fight Wise</a>, a course on how to be less non-confrontational. One I&#8217;d signed up, the default thing was to do the course, however uncomfortable it was. Ultimately being more assertive led to a bunch of downstream changes, including getting out a toxic job that was making me miserable.</p></li></ul><p>Each of these took, what, a minute? Note that none of them fixed the problem &#8211; I still had to move my stuff, have the hard conversation, quit my job. But each was a commitment of a sort, and after saying the words the better outcome was the default rather than something I had to use willpower to choose.</p><p>So set aside an hour, get angry, write the list.
